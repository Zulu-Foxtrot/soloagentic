Decision Fatigue: Building Operational Boundaries in an AI World

The biggest threat to a solo founder isn't a lack of tools—it's having too many choices. Automation should protect your time, not steal it through maintenance.

1. Constrained Stacks

The winning strategy in 2026 is simple: Fewer tools, deeper configurations. Pick your orchestration layer (n8n or pure Python) and stick to it.

2. The 'Human-in-the-loop' Buffer

Never automate the irreversible. Keep humans in the loop for decisions that involve money, reputation, or long-term strategy.
